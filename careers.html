Hospitality Support Staff

As a member of our hospitality support team, you'll help maintain a welcoming and orderly environment at bars and restaurants. Duties include verifying IDs, monitoring guest flow and occupancy, and assisting with general entry procedures — all with a customer-first approach.

Requirements: Friendly and confident personality, strong communication skills, ability to de-escalate situations, and reliability. Previous experience in hospitality or nightlife environments is a plus.
